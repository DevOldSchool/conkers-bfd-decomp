# Legal and provenance policy

This repository accepts independently written contributions where
contributors have the right to submit them under the project's MIT license.
That license does not grant rights to original game code, ROMs, assets,
audio, textures, or other copyrighted game material.

Do not commit ROM images, extracted assets, generated assembly, build
output, or encrypted copies of those materials. Contributors must supply
their own legally obtained ROMs locally.

The project follows a clean-room approach to reconstructing game
functionality, with the contribution exceptions described below. Do not use
original or leaked game source code when preparing contributions.

Independently authored work from another decompilation project may be
accepted where its provenance is documented and the contributor has the
right to submit it under this project's license. Reused third-party material
must meet the permission, license compatibility, and notice requirements
below. These exceptions do not override the restrictions on game source,
ROMs, assets, or other excluded materials.

Do not copy third-party material without permission. For reused material,
identify its source and authors, provide the applicable license or
permission allowing its inclusion, and retain all required copyright and
license notices. Its terms must be compatible with distribution in this
MIT-licensed project. These requirements apply to code, split
configurations, symbols, comments, documentation, generated output, and
metadata. The exclusions above still apply.

External tools and explicitly recorded Git submodules are separate
dependencies and must be pinned to immutable revisions.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to submit
it under the repository license, have disclosed reused third-party material
and its licensing or permission basis, and comply with this policy.
