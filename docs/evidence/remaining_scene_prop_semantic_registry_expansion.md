# Thirty remaining bank-03 descriptive source labels

descriptive labels, not confirmed semantic identities or distinct characters.

[current confidence review](model_name_confidence_review.md) supersedes the

evidence for their stated scope.

SHA-256 pin. No schema, parser, material resolver, runtime consumer, geometry,

## Appearance limits and five canonical corrections

The accepted labels describe inspected stored sources. They do not recover
original names, specific weapon types, actor ownership, controller identity,
functional behavior, collision roles, scene exclusivity or native appearance.
Material words/colours describe inspection pixels. Detail-base, mipmap-base,
lighting, blending, culling, alpha, LOD and raster limitations remain explicit.

- Entry36's inherited square descriptor is approximate; the verified X/Z spans
  are 325/282, not a claim of dimensional equality
- Entries44/45 do not establish traversability, collision, the cause of damage,
  scorching or automatic runtime replacement
- Entry46's apparent grid is a texture on a shallow solid slab, not lattice holes
- Entries64/65 retain distinct payloads and stable first/second-leaf identities,
  despite a shared descriptive name and texture. No placed left/right orientation,
  operating hinge, opening action or behavioral pairing is established
- Entry58's Green launcher label is only a launcher-shaped appearance descriptor.
  Runs1/2 require exact texture agreement across all eleven verified ROM segment-8
  tables at offset0x50. The effective pass, blending and colours remain unresolved.
  Its dark muzzle motif does not establish a geometric opening or launcher behavior
- Entry68's broad one-sided wooden panel is mostly hidden from the original front
  view; rear material and vertex diagnostics support the round-handled door label
- Entry70's launcher interpretation remains held. Black-and-green tube prop is the
  bounded descriptor; its old label survives only as a search/history alias
- Entry85's loop is an alpha-textured two-triangle plane, with six source-colour
  corners under `_SOURCE_COLOR_0`, not active `COLOR_0`. Hanging describes appearance;
  suspension motion, contents, light emission and native alpha/LOD are unproved
- Entry111's blank rear is expected plane back-face culling, not extraction failure
  or proof of gameplay invisibility. The texture does not establish lava substance
- Numbered firearms, platforms and door panels distinguish records. Similarity
  does not establish LOD, phases, animation, runtime selection or shared ownership

Exactly five canonical rows change labels/notes/aliases: entry45 from scorched to
dark ledge; entry79 from hazard to striped panel; entry111 from lava wall to
lava-pattern wall; entry68 to Wooden door with round handle; entry70 to
Black-and-green tube prop. Every old note remains verbatim at the start of its
updated note, with the approved fresh observations appended. Existing aliases,
name/render-case/category identities and all source paths remain. Former labels
are kept as search/history aliases, not corroborating stronger claims. None of
these canonical rows has an `id` field; a null proposal identifier is not a
missing identifier to invent. All other canonical bytes and the entire
validation configuration remain unchanged.

root equivalence, complete consumer spans, exact placements/transforms and
special flags, and entry58's eleven-table texture consensus. Historical digest

Only the thirty exact semantic-name fields, consequent name counts and permitted
registry/canonical input pins may differ; every other model, material, geometry,
scene association, coverage dimension, input and top-level field is equal.
Eight targeted corruptions prove the comparison rejects unrelated changes.
The five canonical label edits themselves grant no new geometry or runtime credit.

All 115 relevant source tests pass without skips. The source patch is checked
against authenticated target preimages, applies cleanly, reproduces every exact
candidate target and reverses to the original bytes. The isolated fixture and

private provenance and symlinked dependencies. No compiler, game build, extractor,

implementation review, integration and game acceptance remain separate gates.
