# Wiki survey and additional gallery identifications

Reviewed on 2026-10-04, starting from the supplied
[Conker's Bad Fur Day article](https://conker.fandom.com/wiki/Conker%27s_Bad_Fur_Day).
This supplements [the weapon review](us_gallery_weapon_names.md).

## Scope and method

Screened all 450 main-namespace article entries in the wiki's two-page
[All Pages index](https://conker.fandom.com/wiki/Special:AllPages), excluding
136 entries marked as redirects. Captured each article's main text, links and
image descriptions. The Bad Fur Day category supplied 211 articles; context-sensitive,
explosive, removed, Spooky and unused-content subcategories were also checked.
Pages about other games were screened for N64 references rather than assumed to
identify Bad Fur Day assets. This is article coverage, not a claim to have
inspected every file, discussion, template, video or media frame on the wiki.

Visually inspected 26 contact sheets covering 1,514 existing gallery preview
records across banks 01, 03, 04 and 09, plus scene assemblies. Two published
cards have no preview. Contact sheets include represented/review variants and
are not a count of unique models. Examined close views and selected N64 wiki
renders/gameplay images for the matches below. Xbox imagery and original
hardware photographs were excluded when identifying N64 exported geometry.
No third-party model or texture payload was imported.

These names describe appearance and wiki context. They are not recovered
source symbols, independently verified runtime ownership, or native-rendering
acceptance. Existing material, animation, neutral-pose, alpha, lighting and
assembly limitations remain unchanged. Earlier labels remain searchable as
`Former label` aliases. All updated cards can be found using
`Wiki review 2026-10-04` in the main gallery search.

## Publication and checks

Updated exactly 43 canonical inspection records: nine new distinct identities,
four tank/hive records with added context, and 30 cheat-keyboard keys.
The main gallery's manifest, HTML and README use the existing renderer and
contain the same names and categories. Its 1,516 visible cards (893 curated,
623 review) are retained. The Weapons tab now has 49 cards.

Before publication and immediately before writing the metadata, checked all
3,030 existing GLB, download and preview files against their published hashes.
All bytes are unchanged. Each of the 43 updated sources independently matched
its source/dependency fingerprint, ROM source evidence and fresh GLB packaging
hash. All non-presentation fields, unedited records and extracted-review
records are unchanged. The byte-pinned semantic registry and confidence sidecar
are unchanged. This updates the existing publication's presentation metadata;
it does not claim a newly validated full-corpus extraction.

The 14 existing gallery tests, metadata schema validation and whitespace checks
passed. Browser verification showed all 43 updated cards through their common
search alias and the expected named cards in the main gallery.

The existing full asset-refresh blocker remains
`ROM guarded model proof differs: rom_ui_material_state`, documented in the
weapon report. No verifier, acceptance decision, validation report or baseline
was altered to bypass it.

Captured article text/link/image inventories, 26 contact sheets, selected wiki
reference screenshots, configuration/manifest snapshots and a per-record
publication audit are retained locally under
`build/assets/models/reference/wiki-survey-20261004/`.
The main gallery is `build/assets/models/inspect/index.html`.
